# Household-weighted Geocene outputs

Generated by the reviewed Geocene analysis using existing cleaned event and
household-day data. Primary and sensitivity exports are processed independently.

## Reading the summaries

An observed household-date requires recorded LPG and/or biomass use.
Unmonitored dates are never inserted as zeros. A household gets one weight within
each table's subset (timepoint, category, receipt window, or overall).

General fuel-specific mean/SD/median minutes and event columns are conditional
on that household's use days for the fuel. Total-stove columns use all monitored
days. The LPG and biomass conditional means therefore do not add to the total.
Each SD is the sample SD of household means, not individual days. Denominator
columns identify eligible households and observed use days. Missing SDs carry
explicit reasons. Single household values have no estimable sample SD.

The existing `pct_exclusive_lpg_days`, `pct_exclusive_biomass_days`, and
`pct_mixed_use_days` fields are compatibility aliases for the explicitly named
`mean_household_pct_*` fields. They are no longer ratios of pooled day counts.
Counts retain their original meanings and cannot be used to reconstruct the
household-weighted percentages by division.

## Linked tables

Files start with `table_descriptive_geocene_household_weighted_`:
- `prevalence.csv`: mean, SD, median of household category fractions and percentages.
- `conditional_use.csv`: distribution of household category-specific daily means, excluding non-users.
- `contributions.csv`: category totals divided by each household's full monitored-day denominator, including zeros for non-users.
- `overall_use.csv`: household daily means including zero LPG/biomass use on observed days.
- `reconstruction.csv`: contribution mean divided by prevalence; not the unweighted conditional-use mean.

Join on `summary_scope`, `window`, `study_arm_overall`, and where appropriate
`stove_use_category`, `fuel`, and `metric`. Window definitions are included.
Scopes cover overall, timepoint, exact post-receipt day, week, existing receipt
month, 30-day windows, legacy receipt intervals, midline intervention months,
post-receipt, and 30+ days after receipt. All-arms rows pool households directly.
Minutes, events, consumed energy, and energy reaching the pot are included.
Energy conversions are unchanged historical formulas, not a new energy model.

Multiply mean prevalence fraction by reconstruction conditional mean to obtain
the mean category contribution. Sum exclusive LPG, exclusive biomass, and
mixed total contributions for the overall total. Mixed LPG and biomass
contributions sum to mixed total. For overall LPG use, add exclusive LPG and
mixed LPG contributions (analogously for biomass). Never add the three mixed
rows as though they represented different household-days.

If a category is absent, its contribution is zero and its reconstruction ratio
is NA. A ratio SD is deliberately not calculated; the file directs readers to
the SDs of household contributions and conditional means. SDs and medians are
computed directly and must not be reconstructed by adding component statistics.

## Figures and reproducibility

Energy boxplots show household category means. Multi-day plots and summary
annotations use household-weighted estimates. Exact-day observations and curves
retain their original household-day definitions. Monthly percentage intervals
are t intervals across household percentages, clipped to 0-100, and are NA for
fewer than two contributing households.

Household-level audit RDS files are written only beneath the dated
`8_restricted/.../qa` directory. Earlier dated outputs are historical versions.
Run the reviewed Geocene entrypoint to refresh only analysis and figure outputs;
the full import/cleaning runner is not required for a weighting change.

Tests:
```sh
Rscript --vanilla tests/test_geocene_pipeline.R
Rscript --vanilla tests/test_geocene_household_weighting.R
Rscript --vanilla tests/test_geocene_external_workdir.R
Rscript --vanilla tests/verify_geocene_weighted_outputs.R
```
The external-working-directory test regenerates the current dated Geocene outputs from existing
cleaned inputs, including both variants, without rerunning survey or PM analyses.
The output verifier reads those CSVs, checks public/private equivalence, and
confirms raw counts and exact-day means have not changed.
